Hi, I’m an architectural designer/researcher who is focused on urban violence, sustainable development, and ecological restoration. Currently located in Paris, where I’m participating in a study abroad program, I have studied in Ottawa and Milan and worked in New York City and Toronto. Originally from Toronto, I apply my experience living in different global cities to create human-centered space that spark joy and excitement. My design ethos focuses on creating spaces with meticulous consideration of aesthetic and pragmatic aspects through the application of rigorous research and creativity.

When I’m not designing, I’m either outside enjoying the fresh air, taking photos of urban experiences, or playing violin in an orchestra.
